Biography

Som Chatterjee is a powerhouse vocalist from West Bengal, India, whose musical path has been shaped by dedication, versatility, and an innate ability to connect with audiences on an emotional level.

Growing up immersed in Bengal's rich cultural and artistic milieu, Som discovered his deep calling in music early. His talent reached millions across the nation through his memorable participation in Zee Bangla Sa Re Ga Ma Pa in 2014, a landmark chapter that earned him widespread critical appreciation and a devoted fanbase.

Today, Som performs at prestigious music festivals, college arenas, and concert halls throughout the country. His repertoire spans soul-stirring Bengali melodies, Hindi Bollywood favorites, Sufi compositions, and high-energy live performance sets.
